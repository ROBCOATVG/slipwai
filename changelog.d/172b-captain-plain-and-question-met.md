**The captain's own parks lead with a sentence in plain English, and a question the harbourmaster has
closed no longer holds its slice.** Two things the plain-cards change left behind.

- **Every park and need the captain writes has a plain line.** A slice out of runs, a gate's attempts
  spent, a session that went quiet or exited, a park its captain agent ordered: each now says first, in one
  or two sentences with no paths or ids, what is wrong and what lets it go on. The captain's own words stay
  under *Details*, and the card no longer says the wording is the agent's own. A need the captain carries
  leads with its own words where they pass the same plain-wording rule, and with a plain sentence where they
  do not. A park that has no needs leads with its plain line too.
- **"Is this question open" is one rule, read by the board, the captain and clearance.** A question is open
  until a decision carries its id or the harbourmaster writes a `question-met` for it. Before, only the
  board read `question-met`: a question closed that way left the board, but clearance still held its slice
  and its capability's gates, and the captain still put it to the skipper. The harbourmaster clears a park
  that waited on such a question by the same rule.

**Catch-up:** `slipwai migrate` carries the changed captain, clearance, harbourmaster and logs scripts. Lines
already written need nothing: a park without a plain line still draws, in the captain's words.
