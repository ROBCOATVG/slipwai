**The draft declares the people the brief names.** A folder handed over with `--brief` may carry a
`people.md`: one `## ` heading per person, a `role:` line and a sentence of what they do. It lands as
`docs/design/people.md` as before, and `commands/draft.md` now has a step that writes one
`specs/<feature>/people/<username>.json` per heading, in the shape the try-it stories sign in as and
`check-try` holds — the heading lowercased as the username, a generated password, the project's realm, the
role, no email — and lists them under `## People` in the specification, so the first stories a slice
writes sign in as people the brief named rather than people the plan invented. A project with no realm
folder has nothing to sign in to: the draft writes the heading and no files, and says so. The draft's
say-back names every person declared.

**Catch-up:** `slipwai migrate` carries the page. A project whose draft has already run writes the files by
hand from `docs/design/people.md`, in the shape `scripts/check-try.py` states, and runs `make check-try`.
