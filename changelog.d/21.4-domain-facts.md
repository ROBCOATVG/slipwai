**The brief's `domain.md` lands as `.specify/domain/brief.md`, where a fact is cited.** A folder handed over
with `--brief` may carry a `domain.md`: one `## ` heading per fact and a line saying where it came from.
It used to land under `docs/design/` with the wireframes, where nothing cites it. Now the folder's
top-level `domain.md` is lifted out of the material and written beside the project's own domain files,
with a first line saying it was handed over, so a decision cites a fact by its heading the way the domain
README says and the skipper reads what the business already knows rather than guessing it. The `brief:`
line `generate` prints says so. A `domain.md` deeper in the folder still travels as material.

**Catch-up:** a project generated from a brief that carried one: `git mv docs/design/domain.md
.specify/domain/brief.md`, and add the first line that says it was handed over, or leave it where it is;
nothing reads either place by name.
