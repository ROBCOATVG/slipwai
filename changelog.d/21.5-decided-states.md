**A handed-over surface page can say who chose each state, and the board keeps it.** `data-decided="person"`
or `data-decided="proposed"` on a state's `<article>` says whether a person asked for the state or the
prototyping tool put it in because every good version of the screen has it. The storyboard's note on the
Voyage window counts them before you approve — *16 states: 10 decided by a person, 6 proposed* — and each
line `mock-states.md` writes ends `(decided by a person)` or `(proposed)`, after the verdict, so
`check-surfaces` and the split read the line as before. `slipwai brief check` says the same counts. A page
that does not say is read and written exactly as it was.

**Catch-up:** `slipwai migrate` brings the mock-up page that tells a redraw to keep the attribute; nothing
else changes for a project whose pages do not carry it.
