**The board has a first page: Describe a product.** A tab beside *Start a project*, never in front of it:
a person with a document, or with nothing, starts a project from the next tab exactly as before. On it, a
conversation draws the product as a thing to tap through, the brief counts itself up beside it in the five
lines the start page will read, and the prototype is embedded from the sketch's own folder. The header
carries the four steps — describe, walk the states, check, hand over — with the one the session is at
marked. The page's routes are its own, hand straight to the session, and serve files from the workspace
and nowhere else. The check in plain words and the hand-over button are the next slice.
