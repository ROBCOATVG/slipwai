**The first page checks the brief in plain words and hands it over with one button.** The five lines the
start page will read are shown with a green mark where the builder can start without asking and an amber
one for what the draft will decide and say — the open questions, or a brief with no document yet. Once
the brief has a document, a button opens *Start a project* with its last field, *Everything else*, already
filled with the brief's folder; the person reads the form and presses Generate as anyone does. The start
page takes that one answer from its address and nothing else about it changes: opened the old way it is
byte for byte the page it was.

**Catch-up:** nothing; a project is not involved.
