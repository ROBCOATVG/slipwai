**The first page asks the right questions, and takes a document a person already has.** The session's
prompt now carries the questions a builder will need answered — what problem and for whom, why now, the
one thing that must get better and what must not get worse, what is out of scope; for each thing it does,
where the information comes from, the rule, what happens when it fails, who may and may not; for each
flow, stopping halfway, steps out of order, two people at once — asked in the person's words, one or two
at a time, before the second drawing. The page with no sketch gains a field for a document, pasted or
chosen as a file, which lands as the brief's own before the first turn; the session reads it first as a
reviewer would, writes every miss under `## Open`, says what it settled, and only then draws. The check
the page already shows is the verdict. The prompt also says a fact's shape, and that the person sees the
prototype beside the conversation. Both doors are optional.
