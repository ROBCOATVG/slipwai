**The draft runs over the Agent Client Protocol from the board, and can ask instead of assume.** Where the
board can launch the project's agent, the draft after `./init` is a session of it rather than a headless
child: the agent's words and tool calls are lines on the receipt as they happen, a permission the board
cannot grant and a question only the person can answer are cards under it, answered there, and a turn under
way has a Stop. Every stage's prompt now carries one rule: ask the person what only they can decide, one
thing at a time, and write it down; assume only what the project already settles. `make draft` from a
terminal, and the draft on a project whose agent has no launch row, run as they did.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the board is served from the installed command.
