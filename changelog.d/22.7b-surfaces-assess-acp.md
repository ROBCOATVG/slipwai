**The surfaces and the assessment run over the Agent Client Protocol from the board, as the draft does.**
Where the board can launch the project's agent, Draw the surfaces and the next-feature assessment are
sessions of it: what the agent reads and writes is on the receipt as it happens, a permission the board
cannot grant and a question only the person can answer are cards under it, and a turn under way has a
Stop. `make surfaces` and `make assess` from a terminal, and both on a project whose agent has no launch
row, run as they did. The model, triage, launch, the adoption steps and the captain's drives still run
headless; each has more than one session in it, and comes in its own change.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the board is served from the installed command.
