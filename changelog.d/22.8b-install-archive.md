**Install an agent from the Settings tab.** A harness distributed as a binary now carries, on its registry
row, the protocol registry's archive for each platform — the URL, the command inside it, and the sha256
where the manifest gives one. On a row this machine cannot launch the page offers *Install*: the archive
is fetched, its sha256 checked against the registry's, and its command unpacked under `~/.slipwai/agents/`,
where the board's launch finds it from then on; *Make ready* is its first start. Where the manifest gives
no checksum — Cursor, Devin and Junie, on 2026-11-05 — the board fetches nothing and shows the link and
the one command to put on PATH. A fault is said on the row. Nothing here installs Node, the board or a
cloud target.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the rows are data, and the board is served from the installed command.
