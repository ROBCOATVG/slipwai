**A project's captains can run over the Agent Client Protocol, switched on from the board.** `sessions`
joins the cruise settings: `print`, the agent's print mode as it always has been, or `protocol`, where a
captain's `/sail` session is one the board can see into — its work shown as it goes in the fleet's stream,
and a stop that stops. The *Who answers* card on the Voyage sets it, through the project's own
`/cruise-settings` script, and the captain reads it. A project that never flips it runs exactly as before;
`SLIPWAI_ACP` in the environment still says first.

**Catch-up:** `slipwai migrate` carries the new setting's explanation into `commands/cruise-settings.md`; a
`.specify/cruise.json` without the key reads as `print`.
