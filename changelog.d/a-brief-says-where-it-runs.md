**A brief can say where the product runs, and the hand-over starts it there.** *Who runs it* takes a third
line, `- Where it runs:` — your AWS account, your Azure subscription, a platform another team runs, or
nowhere yet — and the first page proposes it from what was said or asks. The hand-over then starts from the
quickstart that place names (`aws-customer-product`, `azure-staff-service`, `existing-platform-service` or
`internal-tool`) instead of always the laptop, so the shape the brief's other two lines give reaches
`generate --shape`. It does so only where this machine can reach the place; otherwise it starts on this
machine and says what is missing. On the laptop the page no longer promises a staging environment while
deploying nowhere: it says what the build will be when it goes live.

**Catch-up:** none. A brief without the line starts on this machine, as before.
