**A prototype and a storyboard can be sent to somebody who is not at the board.** Both were served from
this machine only, so there was nothing to link to, and a storyboard is a folder: its file alone is a page
of empty frames. *Save a copy to send* — under the prototype on the first page, and under the storyboard on
the Voyage — downloads one HTML file with everything the page shows inside it: stylesheets and scripts
written in, pictures as data, each framed page in its frame, each linked page drawn at the end. A strip on
the copy says what it is and when it was made; a storyboard's says where its verdict stood. A copy carries
only what the board would have served for that page, names what it left out, and says how much of it still
loads from the internet. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is written to the project; a verdict is still
given on the board.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the board is served from the installed command.
