**A decision the chart took is said as one, not asked.** The chart recorded its context decision and then
posted it as a question, so every launch stopped a person to confirm what the stage had already decided
with its reasons written down. It posts a `decision` line now, worded with the way to overturn it, which
the board lists under *Read and mark read*; a `question` is kept for a seam the evidence cannot settle. The
box under a question answers that question — the answer is the `decision` that closes it, written by the
person, as `decided_by: human` — where it used to write a note beside it and leave the question standing.

**Catch-up:** a project regenerates its command pages with `slipwai migrate` to get the chart's new words;
nothing else changes.
