**A fairway that is waiting for its first slice is "not started", not "stalled".** A launch writes its
decisions into the fairway they are about, so a fairway whose slices all wait on another's had lines in
its log and no captain writing more. After the stall bound the Fleet tab's headline read "Stalled: nights
stopped writing without a word" over a fleet that was working. A fairway is stalled only once a captain
has claimed a slice of it or written a heartbeat.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the board is served from the installed command.
