**A further feature can be handed over on the board.** A project with its first specification now always has
the **Next features** tab on its Voyage window, with ideas waiting or without, and on it **Hand over a
further feature**: say what it is, or attach its document (Markdown or plain text, up to 256 KB), and the
agent the Settings tab names reads it, asks what it needs to, and says when it is one clear idea. **Capture
this idea** then writes `docs/intake/<slug>/` exactly as `/sail` would, and the idea waits for **Continue the
next feature** as before: capturing does not confirm. The conversation runs over the Agent Client Protocol,
reads the project and cannot change it, and is picked up again when the window is reopened. Before this the
first step of a second feature was a terminal, and the button that said so was not drawn until an idea
existed.

**Catch-up:** `slipwai migrate` brings `docs/intake/README.md` and the docs index their new wording; the
board itself needs nothing, since the tab is the app's and not the project's.
