**The Voyage window keeps itself current from one poll, and says what waits on you in one place.** The page
asks `/live.json` every few seconds and swaps only the bands that changed — Needs a look, the work, what the
streams asked, the feedback points, What to expect, the Fleet tab and the Decisions count — never closing a
card a person has open or a box they are typing in. Behind it the board reads a project once and keeps the
read for about two seconds for every poll and window, drops it on any action a person takes, asks the
project's clearance script once a read rather than twice, and asks the login shell once for the board's
life: one poll cycle went from about two seconds of server time to about a quarter of one, and to a few
milliseconds while the read is kept. `/now.json` and `/work.json` are gone.

*Waiting on you* is one list in two groups — holding work up (a slice parked on you, a stage's card, the
button the run waits on, a log nobody can read) and when you have time (a capability ready to see, a
feedback proposal to confirm) — read by the strip, which opens on both groups, the Work tab's count and the
Waiting on you column alike. A note nobody has read and a slice paused until a set time are not counted. The
Now strip is *Needs a look*: only a slice stuck on a task, going in circles or gone quiet, a refused merge,
and a red trunk, each linked to where to look, and one line when nothing is wrong. The work has a Cards /
Compact switch, remembered per browser. The Fleet tab is the machinery: sessions in use and who takes the
next, today's tokens against the budget, trunk, a row per process, the settings and Stop; what the streams
asked of you moved under the work.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the board is the app's, and an existing project's next Voyage window has it.
