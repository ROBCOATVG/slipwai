**Hand-over from a brief is a confirmation, not a questionnaire.** A person who has just finished a brief
on the first page has answered everything in plain words, and the start page met them with its seventeen
questions. Now it reads the brief and says, in a few lines, what it will make: the project named for the
sketch, a browser app where the brief drew surfaces, who signs in from *Who runs it*, the service's purpose
from the brief's first paragraph, the agent from the Settings tab, and for the builder's own choices the
smallest start — one service on this machine, a real store in one file, nothing to install. One button
makes it; *Change something* opens the whole form, prefilled. The start page reached from its own tab is
unchanged.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the board is served from the installed command.
