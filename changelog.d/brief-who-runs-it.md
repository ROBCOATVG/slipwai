**The brief says who runs it, and the first build is sized from that.** Two lines under `## Who runs it`
in the requirements document — who uses it, staff or customers, and what an afternoon without it is, an
annoyance or an emergency — in the person's words and none of ours. The first page works them out from
what was said and drawn and writes them `(proposed)`, which the check shows amber; where it cannot tell it
asks the two questions in plain words, one at a time. A staff tool whose outage is an annoyance starts as
a skiff, one environment on the smallest compute; anything else as a liner. The start page passes
`--shape` from the lines; a brief without them starts small, as before. `slipwai brief check` gains the
line *runs for*.

**Catch-up:** nothing; a brief folder made before this has no such lines and starts small as it did.
