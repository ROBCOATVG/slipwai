**Feedback's New scope opens the hand-over conversation.** Confirming a feedback item as **New scope** on the
board used to write its words straight under `docs/intake/`, with nobody asking whether they were one feature
and no way to send a document along. It now opens **Hand over a further feature** on the **Next features**
tab with the item's words as your first: the same conversation with the project's agent, over the Agent
Client Protocol, with the same limits, and a document attached as to any other turn. The item stays a
proposal until **Capture this idea**, and is then recorded as having become that idea, as before
(`became: docs/intake/<slug>`). One hand-over is open at a time, so a second item waits for the first.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the door is the board's, and an item already confirmed as new scope keeps the idea it
became.
