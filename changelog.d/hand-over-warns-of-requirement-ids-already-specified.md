**The hand-over warns where a document names requirement ids the project already specifies.** When a
document attached on the **Next features** tab names ids that a specification under `specs/` already lists —
`FR-003`, `SC-001` and the like — the card lists them beside **Capture this idea**, each with the document it
is in and the specification that holds it, and says so again when the idea is captured. It is a match on the
ids only, and the warning says that: the board does not compare what the two say, and two features can both
number from one. Nothing is refused. With no ids in the document, no match, or no specification, nothing is
said.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the warning is the board's and reads the specifications a project already has.
