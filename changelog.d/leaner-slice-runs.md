MINOR

**A slice costs less to run.** One lean-coffee-club slice read 113 million tokens of its own conversation to
write 224 thousand, because a single session carried it from its example map to its merge and was resumed the
next morning where it stopped. Now every run of the ladder the fleet dispatches is a session of its own, and
a slice is worked in parts: the session ends its turn once the tasks are written and again once the review's
findings are closed, and the next part starts at once in a new session that reads the slice from its files.
Those handovers cost no attempt. A person's own `/sail` runs through as before.

**A slice is walked twice at most.** `walks` in `harbour.json`, two where it names none: the walk, and one
more to see what it sent back put right. After that, a finding that is not critical is fixed or written into
the careen as a task, and the verdict is recorded with `deck_line.py demo --verdict accepted --stowed <n>`.
A third walk is opened only by saying what is critical: `benchmark.py start <dir> demo critical="…"`.

**A pre-launch product is read once per capability, not once per slice.** `open` — what a pre-launch product
merges under — ran converge, the gaps trace, the design review and the hand's walk on every slice, and
attacked a slice that widened authorisation, identity or money, because its merges are visible. They are
visible to nobody: the product has no users yet. Those readings now wait for the capability in every release
mode. A project whose merges do reach somebody at once asks for them with `"readings": "slice"` in
`harbour.json`, and even then a slice that only shows — a view over events other slices write — waits for the
capability.

**The fleet lands a hand-back that names a local service's file.** A slice that needs something under
`docker/` — a client in the local sign-in realm, a database's init script — hands it back as a need, and the
mender now takes it as it takes `.env.example` and `docs/`. And the pages say what the scope gate always
allowed: the manifest and lock of the service that owns a slice are the slice's to change.

**The walk and the delegated gaps trace run on the `fast` role** in a new project's `.specify/models.json`.

**A merged slice's containers and its local branch go with its worktree.**

**Catch-up:** `slipwai migrate` carries the scripts and pages. An existing `.specify/models.json` is the
project's own and keeps its roles: to move the two stages, set `"demo-hand": "fast"` and
`"gaps-lookout": "fast"` under `stages`. Add `"walks": <n>` to `harbour.json` only to change the two, and
`"readings": "slice"` only to keep every slice read. A pre-launch project stops reading each slice at its
next `/sail` with nothing to do.
