**A prototype that does not run says so, and can be played at full width.** On the first page a session could
draw a prototype, say "tap through it on the right", and leave a white box: one name a browser page may not
declare at the top of a script (`function top`) and the browser drops the whole script without a word. The
page now says under the prototype what failed and on which line — a script that would not parse or that threw,
or a page that loaded and drew nothing — with one button that says it to the session in your conversation.
Nothing is said to the session until you press it. The sketch's instructions tell the session to keep its
script out of the page's globals. Under the prototype there is also *Show it full width*, which the browser
remembers, and *Open the prototype in a full window*. Typing in the conversation and pressing Enter now takes
you to what you just said, wherever you had scrolled.

**Catch-up:** nothing to do; a sketch already drawn gains all of this the next time its page is opened.
