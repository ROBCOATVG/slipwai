PATCH

**A long answer in a sketch is no longer cut off at fifteen minutes without a word.** A document a person
brought is reviewed, corrected and drawn in one answer, and a long one took ten minutes over a single reply:
the answer was stopped at fifteen with the drawing half written, and the page showed a conversation that had
simply stopped. An answer may now take an hour, the Stop button ends one sooner, and one stopped at the limit
says so and says how to pick it up: *say `carry on`*.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the board is served from the installed command.
