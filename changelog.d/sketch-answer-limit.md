**An answer in a sketch or the console never ends in silence.** A turn that had already spoken could end
with nothing on the page, and the conversation had simply stopped: stopped at the time limit, crashed half
way through, ended without a word, or lost by the board's own reader. Each now says what happened:

- stopped at the limit: *This answer was stopped after 60 minutes, before it was finished*;
- the agent ended early: its status, whether the answer was finished, and the last lines it printed that
  were not its stream — which is where an agent says what went wrong;
- it ended well and said nothing: *The session ended without an answer*;
- the board failed while hearing it: what failed, and the agent is stopped rather than left running unseen;
- the Stop button: *Stopped.*

In a sketch, an answer that had begun adds that what it wrote is kept and that `carry on` picks it up.

**A sketch's answer may take an hour**, not fifteen minutes. A document a person brought is reviewed,
corrected and drawn in one answer, and a long one took ten minutes over a single reply.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the board is served from the installed command.
