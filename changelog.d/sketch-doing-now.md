and a block can be ten minutes of thinking or a whole prototype typed as one file: the page said *Working…*

what is happening, *Thinking…* or *Writing prototype/index.html, 48 KB so far*, and on every machine it says
for how long once the answer has been under way a minute: *· 6 min*.
