**A stage that has ended can be answered on its card.** A stage run over the Agent Client Protocol that
asked its question in its words, rather than on a card, ended its turn with nobody able to answer it: the
session closed and the card had no box. The *Made* card now carries a reply box once such a stage has
ended, as the first page does. A reply is one more turn of the same session, loaded from where the stage
kept it, its lines on the same receipt; the replayed conversation is kept off it. After the board is
restarted, a project with a kept stage session offers the same box on the most recent one.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the board is served from the installed command.
