**Start from a PRD and Start from a codebase open at once, whatever the machine's tools take to answer.**
Both pages asked `git`, `make`, `python` and `node` for their versions before they were sent, so on a slow
or cold machine a person looked at an empty tab for as long as the slowest of them took — twelve seconds on
one Windows machine. The pages are now sent first. The line that says what the machine lacks reads
*Checking what this machine has…* until each tool has answered and then says what it always said, or
nothing where the machine has it all; *This machine* on Settings fills in row by row the same way. The
tools are asked once for every open page rather than once per load, and a good answer is kept five
minutes. A tool that says nothing for fifteen seconds is reported as not having answered, with a reload to
ask it again, rather than as missing.

**Catch-up:** nothing; it is the board's own pages, and the app has them as soon as it is updated.
