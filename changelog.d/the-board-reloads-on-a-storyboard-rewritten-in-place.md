**An open board window shows a storyboard that was rebuilt, without a reload by hand.** The window reloads
itself when the files its page is made of change, and it read the storyboard's pages by name only. A
conversation with the storyboard that rebuilt `storyboard.html`, redrew a page or closed the review's
Blockers left every name as it was, so the window went on showing the first drawing while the stage said the
storyboard was ready. The page now also reads when the mock-up pages, `research.md` and `review.md` were last
written. It holds the reload while a turn of conversation is still writing, in this window or another one,
and reloads once when that turn ends; it still never reloads over words somebody is typing.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the window is the app's, not the project's.
