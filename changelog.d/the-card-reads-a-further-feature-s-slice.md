**A further feature's slice moves along its card as it is built.** There is one chart per project, written
under the first feature, and the board read every slice's example map, plan, tasks and stage record under
that feature. A slice of a later feature keeps them under its own, so its card showed `Examples` through the
whole build while its own lines said it was implementing and being reviewed. The board now finds a slice's
files under whichever feature holds them.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the board is the app's, not the project's.
