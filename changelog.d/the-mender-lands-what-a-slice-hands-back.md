**The fleet lands what a slice hands back, wherever a slice may not write it.** The mender took a need only
where its file was one of six places: `.gitignore`, `.env.example`, `docs/`, `packages/`, `design-system/` and
`docker/`. Anything else a slice handed back was nobody's: the slice may not write it, the mender might not,
and it stood on the board for a person. lean-coffee-club had sixteen such needs open after two days, among
them a compose file's environment line, each fairway's `careen.md`, a flag's declaration and a service's build
file. That list was never meant as the whole of it.

A need's change may now land anywhere, but for four things:

- a gate, what runs one, or what decides what an agent may do: `scripts/`, `tools/`, the `Makefile`,
  `harbour.json`, `project.json`, `.specify/`, `.slipwai/`, `.githooks/`, `.github/`, `agents/`, `commands/`
  and `skills/` — as before, with one exception, `skills/run-the-app/`, which is the project's own record of
  how its app is run;
- the cloud under `infra/`, which stays a person's, other than a flag's declaration in
  `infra/service/flags.auto.tfvars`;
- a slice's own folder under `specs/<feature>/slices/`;
- source and tests, which are a slice's work and not a hand-back.

A need still has to carry a `check` naming the file, and be written for the fleet (`by: fleet`), or for a
person while `/cruise` is on.

**Catch-up:** `slipwai migrate` carries `scripts/agents/mender.py` and the brief; start the fleet again. Needs
already on the board are taken at the harbourmaster's next pass.
