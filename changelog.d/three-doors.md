**The board's three doors are named for where you start.** *Start from an idea* is the conversation that
draws a prototype and writes the brief; *Start from a document* is the form that takes a requirements
document or a brief; *Start from a codebase* installs the method around code that exists. They sit in the
bar in that order, which is the order a person arrives. *Describe a product*, *Start a project* and *Adopt
a repository* were three verbs that did not say which one was yours.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the board is served from the installed command.
