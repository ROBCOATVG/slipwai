**The way to the voyage is on the page after a generate.** *Generate* opens the voyage in another tab when
the project is made, and a browser only lets a page open a tab for a few seconds after the click that
asked. A generate takes longer, so the tab was refused without a word and the run went on where nobody was
looking. The page now carries a link to the voyage under what the generate said, and says whether the tab
opened.
