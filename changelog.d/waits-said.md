**What waits on you is said where you will see it.** The Voyage and the first page each put a strip under
their title the moment something waits on a person — the specification ready to read and draw, a storyboard
to approve, a question on a card, a slice parked on you, the brief ready to hand over — with the way to it
beside the words, and the count on the tab's title and on the installed board's badge. A switch in the
corner, *Tell me when it waits on me*, asks the browser once and from then on a wait that begins while you
are in another window is a notification; off until you throw it, and never asked for on your behalf.

**Catch-up:** nothing; the board is served from the installed command.
